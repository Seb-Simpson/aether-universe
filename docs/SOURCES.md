## Interpretation and limits

- Quantities are educational reference values, not live telemetry. Distances are rounded descriptive values from NASA, not ephemerides for the current date.
- `Equatorial diameter` and `mean radius` are intentionally distinct. Saturn's equatorial diameter is not its mean diameter.
- Temperatures vary by location, illumination and depth. Labels identify approximations and Rhea's sunlit value.
- Saturn's rotation period is an approximate value from NASA; its gaseous atmosphere has differential rotation.
- No claim of discovered life is made. Enceladus's ocean and Titan's chemistry are scientifically interesting; life has not been detected there.
- Moon counts are deliberately omitted: confirmed counts change over time.
- The rendered system uses illustrative body sizes, orbital spacing, orientation and lighting. It is not physically to scale, an orbital simulation, a navigational tool, or a view of the sky at a particular time. Scene camera tilt is a presentation choice, separate from the sourced axial-tilt fact.
- Star positions form a decorative background rather than an astronomical star catalogue.

When adding facts, use a primary source, preserve units and rounding, and supply an explicit source id. Recheck changing facts before publishing.
